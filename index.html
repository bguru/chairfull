Frequently asked questions

What does ChairFull do?

ChairFull sends automated SMS reminders to dental patients and offers cancelled slots to your waitlist, so fewer appointments go empty.

Who is ChairFull for?

Independent dental clinics and small dental groups that lose revenue to no-shows and last-minute cancellations.

How long does setup take?

About 15 minutes. ChairFull connects to your calendar, and reminders start the same day.
